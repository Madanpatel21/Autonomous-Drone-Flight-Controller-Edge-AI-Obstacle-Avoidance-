# Schematic Review Checklist

- Power entry and protection reviewed
- Regulator sequencing reviewed
- MCU decoupling and reset reviewed
- SWD/JTAG accessible
- Crystal/clock network validated
- Sensor buses have pull-ups where required
- UART/SPI/I2C assignments documented
- ESC outputs protected and clearly labeled
- Battery sensing scaled and protected
- Test points provided
- Connector pinouts documented
- Ground strategy reviewed
- DRC/ERC clean
