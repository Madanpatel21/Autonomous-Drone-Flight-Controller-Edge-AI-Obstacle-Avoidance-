# PCB Review Checklist

- Stackup selected
- High-current paths sized
- Analog sensing separated from noisy switching paths
- IMU placement and orientation documented
- Vibration/mechanical mounting considered
- Ground return paths continuous
- Decoupling close to pins
- RF/GNSS antenna constraints respected
- Programming/test access preserved
- Silkscreen polarity and connector labels present
- DRC clean
- 3D clearance reviewed
- Manufacturing capability checked
