# Manufacturing Release Checklist

Every line carries a **status** and either **evidence** (an artifact that exists
and is digest-pinned in [`RELEASE_MANIFEST.json`](RELEASE_MANIFEST.json)) or a
**gate** (the requirement or facility blocker that stops it, checked live by
`12_manufacturing/tools/audit_release_manifest.py`). A line may not be blank and
may not be marked done-by-assertion: that is the failure mode this rewrite exists
to remove.

## How this checklist is verified

```
python 12_manufacturing/tools/audit_release_manifest.py
```

runs in the regression suite as its own step. It fails on: a digest-pinned
artifact that changed without a revision bump, a gated item whose gate is
unresolvable or has since been marked MET, a facility gate whose probe shows the
blocker is gone, a `tbd` BOM row without a gate, a stub file listed as evidence,
or any drift between the FC and GS protocol constants.

**Not claimed:** this checklist being green does not mean a board can be built.
Items 1–9, 11, 15, 17, 19 and 21 are open on hardware, and the package is
software-complete / fabrication-incomplete by construction.
